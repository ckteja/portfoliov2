    FRAME 01About

    Research-driven, deployment-minded.

    My work sits where a model stops being a notebook result and starts being a system. That means the unglamorous parts: evaluating honestly, cutting a model down to fit the hardware it will actually run on, and instrumenting it so you know when it degrades.

    Most of it has been video: recognising human actions across frames, holding identities through occlusion, and running detection on a Jetson Nano rather than a datacentre GPU. Three of those projects became IEEE ICAECT papers, which forced a level of rigour in evaluation I've kept since.

    At Passau I've been extending that into foundation models and end-to-end systems, putting SAM, YOLO and ViT in a single pipeline with zero-shot and few-shot learning and live monitoring over the inference stack.

    Alongside that I'm building i-Brick with a small team. Different domain, same problem shape as the Jetson work: constrained hardware, unreliable networks, and a model whose output somebody has to trust enough to spend money on.

    FRAME 02Venture

    i-Brick: sensing the wall from the inside.

    By the time a damp patch is visible on a wall, it has been growing for months. Damp is the most common building defect in Europe, and it is still diagnosed by eye, after the damage is done. I'm building i-Brick with a small team to move that diagnosis months earlier, while the fix is still ventilation rather than structural repair.

    Sensor nodes sit inside the wall build-up rather than sampling room air, so they measure what the structure is actually doing. They run autonomously through network outages and report when they reconnect. A risk engine turns the readings into one 0–100 score with plain-language reasoning, not a spreadsheet, delivered as a live dashboard, alerts, and engineer-ready reports.

    The design decision I care most about: when a condition hasn't been measured, the system reports it as unassessed. It never reports unmeasured as fine. Honest uncertainty is worth more than a confident number, particularly when an insurer or a housing provider is going to act on it.
